วีธรา บูธีค โฮเทล · A home away from home

Since 2016, Veethara has welcomed travellers, families and business guests to Udon Thani. Tucked just off Udondussadee Road near the Clock Tower, the hotel is central yet quiet — an easy base for exploring the city on foot or by car.
